What does a D&D adventure look like?

Penned by Yasha Mikolajczak

Dungeons and Dragons has been around since 1974, and has been through many iterations, since. Even though it's gained popularity within the last few years, there are still many who haven't experienced what a typical game or "story arc" looks like. As you scroll through, you'll see the story beats that were hit in this arc and how the these players and their characters, defeated "The Cerulean Song". This adventure is an actual adventure that I had developed for my friends. Consider this both an archive and a way to learn what D&D is all about!

Our adventure starts in the way most adventures do. In a tavern, where we meet our characters. Here in the town of Heartwell, we meet our four adventurers who we will be following on this journey!
